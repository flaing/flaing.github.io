Isaac Newton (1642–1727) is a favorite authority figure for modern internet debunkers because of his late-life book, The Chronology of Ancient Kingdoms Amended.

  • The Timeline War: In the 17th century, European chronologists stretched Egyptian and Greek histories back thousands of years to align them with various ancient myths. Newton thought these dates were wildly exaggerated.
