💡 Why Did Movable Type Spread Faster in the West?

Though China invented movable type 400 years earlier, the technology caused a much faster, explosive industrial revolution in Europe. This was due to the linguistic structure of the languages:

  • Chinese (Logographic): Features 3,000 to 5,000 commonly used characters. Therefore, ancient Chinese publishers often preferred woodblock printing for large book projects.
  • Western Languages (Alphabetic): English, German, and Latin require only 26 letters, plus capitalization and punctuation. Gutenberg only needed to mass-produce a few dozen distinct letter molds to print any text imaginable. This drastically lower technical barrier allowed the printing press to spread across Europe like wildfire.
